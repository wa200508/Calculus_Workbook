## Findings and corrections

All 52 original exercises were analytically rederived, including their
statements, hints, complete calculations, checks, and domain claims. No incorrect
final answer was found. The review did find incorrect or incomplete explanations:

- I4-001 incorrectly said the positive-kernel interchange does not extend to
  the zero-parameter endpoint. Tonelli still applies, with value positive
  infinity. The correction distinguishes interchange from finite integrability.
- Reference chain rules omitted sufficient differentiability conditions in
  several entries. Coordinate partials alone do not justify a path chain rule;
  the audit gives an explicit counterexample whose actual slope differs from
  the formal chain-rule value.
- The uniform-series integration statement needed integrable summands. It now
  gives the sufficient case of continuous summands on a finite closed interval.
- Parameter differentiation now requires an integrable slice in the same
  neighborhood where derivative domination applies. Integrability at an
  unrelated parameter does not establish local integrability.
- I3-008 and the methods reference conflated angular seam identification with
  a degenerate coordinate Jacobian. The revised descriptions distinguish them.
- The oscillatory cosine-tail estimate is calculated explicitly, the panel
  observation parameter is declared dimensionless, and local assumptions for
  logarithmic derivatives and constrained stationary points are made explicit.

The [review ledger](BETA_REVIEW.md) links the six baseline audit notes, with
per-problem derivations, counterexamples, checks, and limits of the review.
