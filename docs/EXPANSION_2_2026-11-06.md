## Review findings and repairs

The independent reviewers derived expected results before comparing the new
solutions. All twenty final derivations passed that comparison. Additional
readers examined the principal-value and Fresnel arguments and the integration
checker.

- I3-015 originally stated the real dilogarithm derivative for every nonzero
  argument in its defined domain, which included one. Review restricted the
  formula to arguments below one and explained that the finite endpoint value
  has a divergent left derivative. Its integral value and endpoint limits were
  already correct.
- Complete elliptic integrals consistently use the parameter $m$, with the
  conversion to DLMF's modulus $\sqrt m$ stated explicitly. This preserves the
  constants in the ring asymptotic and the nearly circular arc-length formula.
- The first PDF margin check found an overflowing inline speed formula in
  I4-014. Displaying the full derivative-norm equation removed the overflow.
- A numerical-check review replaced a tautological endpoint-constant check
  with quadrature of the ordinary logarithmic endpoint kernel and an explicit
  tail bound. The maintained integration check count remains 197.

No mathematical finding remains unresolved within these reviewed additions.
Analytical reasoning and numerical samples remain separate kinds of evidence.
